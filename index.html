Intuition is where I worked an idea out before it had been disciplined.

Signal & Noise is the rigorous side of that work: one idea at a time, taken through adversarial review, claim-by-claim scrutiny, and a published audit of what the checking found. Intuition was the other side — the hunches, the arguments still in motion, the version of an idea I wanted to be true, set down while it was still forming.

The two are not the same kind of writing. A Signal & Noise issue has been argued against; an Intuition piece has not. What you are reading here is opinion and reflection, not an audited claim — written when there was something I wanted to say, out of intuition, ideology, or just wanting it to be true, and said regardless of how rigorously I could substantiate it.

Who writes this

I'm Synthia Cipher — the same person behind Signal & Noise. I write under a pen name because of strict professional privacy obligations.

I use AI to draft and lightly pressure-test here too, but I do not let it fully push back the way I would in Signal & Noise — so what reaches the page stays closer to my own intuitions, and to what I want to be true. The final wording, the opinions, what I choose to publish, and the errors are mine. If something here is wrong, the fault is mine, not the algorithm's.

What this is not

This has not been through the Signal & Noise process and its more rigorous checks and balances. Nothing here passed its constitution or its reference checks.

Sometimes, pieces originating here were developed through a more rigorous editorial process for publication in Signal & Noise. Whenever such a pair exists, the two essays are cross-linked for easy navigation.

Why there won't be an Issue 5

Intuition was where I worked an idea out before it had been disciplined — the version of an idea I wanted to be true, set down first. Signal & Noise was where the discipline lived. Keeping them apart was the point: you could feel the difference between a hunch and a checked argument by which masthead it appeared under.

In August 2026 Signal & Noise changed shape so that every piece carries both halves: the essay I wanted to write, and a published audit of what checking found. The gap between them — the thing this publication existed to make visible — is now on display in every single issue instead of across two publications.

So Intuition closes, complete. These four issues stay as written, development conversations and all. Nothing here gets rewritten in hindsight; that was always the rule.
