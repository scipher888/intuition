AI editorial process · paired-format diagnosis · June 16
The Gary Marcus idea is strong because it gives the pair a real test. Your Intuition piece can say: “I emotionally read these warnings like automobile death-toll posts: true, serious, but maybe socially absorbed as accepted cost.” Then the stricter version has to ask the harder question: when does “accepted risk” become genuine acceptance, and when is it just normalization before governance catches up? My read: Gary probably does well under the stricter process if the question is technical substance. The place he may lose ground is not “is this false?” but “does this change adoption behavior, regulation, or public legitimacy at this stage?” That distinction is exactly the format’s advantage. And yes, AI ending up defending Gary Marcus would be excellent, but only if it is not played as irony. The real power is: **the constrained process should be allowed to embarrass the Intuition piece.** If Intuition says “culture moved on,” the stricter audit may answer: “Maybe. But here is where he is still pointing at a live structural risk.”
